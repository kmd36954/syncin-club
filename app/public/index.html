How it works

Simple. Verified. Professional.

Four steps from signup to your first shared commute.

1

Create your profile

Sign up with your professional details. Every member is verified before accessing the network.

2

Post or find a journey

Host a journey with seats to offer, or broadcast your route and let verified hosts find you.

3

Review the match

See the co-traveler's full profile, company, and designation before accepting any connection.

4

Connect on WhatsApp

On match, both parties connect directly on WhatsApp. Fuel costs agreed privately between members.
